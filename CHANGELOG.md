# Changelog

What changed in each release of Lieutenant Fizz 2, in plain language for players. It is written by hand in the release PR, and it is the source of the release text and of `CHANGES.TXT` on the floppy. The record of each merged pull request is `WORKLOG.md`.

## Unreleased

- Show a word where things happen, as Lieutenant Fizz 1 does: `fzzt`, `click - no fizz`, `fizzled`, `bonk`, `whoa!`, `ta-da!`, the red, blue and green `gumdrop`, `clunk` for doors and head bumps, `boing` and `crunch`, in Fizz 1's colours, on the Web and on DOS, and hidden by the Captions setting (which still hides only the words). A repeat of the same word within a fifth of a second is left out, six words show at most, and a word far outside the picture is not shown. A new table in `fizz-slice-view` also names the sound each event will use (ids 0 to 14); no sound is added yet. The chicken's own caption and all hashes are unchanged.
- The gallery at the top of the level is now a small playground: a snack nook with cheezies, a chocolate bar, a cookie and a blue key on a shelf you reach on the pogo stick, a blue door in front of the exit, and a spot to bump your head. Taking a snack, bumping, bouncing, the empty blaster click and opening the blue door all happen in play now, though only the chicken's cluck makes a sound so far. The built-in test run is longer (1,681 steps, about 28 seconds on DOS).
