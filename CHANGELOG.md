# Changelog

What changed in each release of Lieutenant Fizz 2, in plain language for players. It is written by hand in the release PR, and it is the source of the release text and of `CHANGES.TXT` on the floppy. The record of each merged pull request is `WORKLOG.md`.

## Unreleased

- Every word has a sound, as in Lieutenant Fizz 1, and so does the jump: `fzzt`, `fizzled`, `bonk`, `whoa!`, `ta-da!`, the gumdrops, `clunk`, `click - no fizz`, `boing`, a jump and `crunch` (eleven new effects, written as Undertone voices with FM and pitch steps; the chicken keeps its four clucks). They come from where they happen, panned and softened like the cluck, on the Web, and on DOS through the Sound Blaster's stereo pair, the AdLib's one voice or the PC speaker, whichever the computer has. They play with the Captions setting on or off, a sound is not left out when its word is, and on DOS the newest sound cuts the one before it off, as in Fizz 1. The sounds are first drafts and nobody has listened to them yet. On the Web, the panel you open with `H` has a new list, "Hear an effect", that plays any of the fifteen by itself. `FIZZ2.EXE` grows by about 10 KB (the sounds are 6 KB of that). All hashes are unchanged.
- The fizzy drink can in the Paused and Options screens is purple now, the grape colour of Fizz in this game, with white bubbles, on DOS and on the Web (it was Fizz 1's red). Only the can's colours change; all hashes are unchanged.
- `FIZZ2.EXE /BENCH` also times the picture with six captions on screen and prints the extra cost; with `/AUDIO` it does the same while effects play.
- Show a word where things happen, as Lieutenant Fizz 1 does: `fzzt`, `click - no fizz`, `fizzled`, `bonk`, `whoa!`, `ta-da!`, the red, blue and green `gumdrop`, `clunk` for doors and head bumps, `boing` and `crunch`, in Fizz 1's colours, on the Web and on DOS, and hidden by the Captions setting (which still hides only the words). A repeat of the same word within a fifth of a second is left out, six words show at most, and a word far outside the picture is not shown. A new table in `fizz-slice-view` also names the sound each event uses (ids 0 to 14). The chicken's own caption and all hashes are unchanged.
- The gallery at the top of the level is now a small playground: a snack nook with cheezies, a chocolate bar, a cookie and a blue key on a shelf you reach on the pogo stick, a blue door in front of the exit, and a spot to bump your head. Taking a snack, bumping, bouncing, the empty blaster click and opening the blue door all happen in play now, each with its sound. The built-in test run is longer (1,681 steps, about 28 seconds on DOS).
