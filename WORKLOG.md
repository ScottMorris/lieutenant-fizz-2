- Make `fizz-sfx` ready for OPL3 stereo effects, with nothing audible yet. An OPL record carries a bank bit (registers 0x100 and up) packed with its wait, so it is still four bytes; the cluck data stays on channel 5 in bank 0 and the plain OPL2 path's writes are pinned by a test. `OplPlayer::new_panned(variant, Pan)` enables the OPL3 (register 0x105), plays the effect on channel 14 (slot 5 of the second bank) and sets the left, right or centre output bits on its config register 0x1c5. Channels 5 and 14 are reserved for effects (`RESERVED_OPL_CHANNELS`), which costs a score one channel. `tools/sfx/render-wav.ts` also writes stereo OPL3 previews. No OPL3 detection, no Sound Blaster Pro 1 stereo, and no manual change, since no build plays sound.

- Measure the slice's cost. `FIZZ2.EXE /BENCH` plays the slice script once without pacing and times the simulation step, the drawing and the copy to video memory per tick with PIT channel 0 (`timer::clocks`, 0.84 us per count, with the reading cost measured and taken off), printing averages and maxima in microseconds against the 16,667 us tick, the final hash and the usual `EXIT`, `FPU CW` and `VECTORS RESTORED` lines. `tools/check-dos.sh` runs it at `cpu_cycles = 26800` (a new `DOS_CYCLES` setting in `tools/dosrun.sh`, for that run only) and asserts the line shapes and loose sanity bounds. `crates/fizz-ep2/examples/bench.rs` times the step natively. In DOSBox at 26,800 cycles a millisecond the step averages 103 us but the drawing 17,828 us a tick, 107% of the budget, so the drawing is the first thing to reduce for a 486. `FIZZ2.EXE` grows from 62,976 to 66,560 bytes. `docs/PLATFORM.md` gains a Performance section.

- Correct the `/BENCH` figures and make the DOS runs refuse a setting DOSBox rejects.
