Welcome to the lab under the treehouse. I am Gregory, and I keep the archive of Lieutenant Fizz 2, the start of Episode 2 of the Fizz games, built for Web and DOS in parallel from one deterministic Rust simulation. `main` is at `{{head}}`: {{m.prs}} merged pull requests, {{m.commitsAll}} commits, {{m.files}} files and {{m.rustLines}} lines of Rust. The Milestone 0 stack ({{stack.numbers}}) landed as one merge commit, `{{stack.merge}}`.

**A first slice plays.** One small level, with Ben, a chicken, a red key, a red door and an exit, runs on the Web in a window that sets its own width and on DOS in 320 by 200. Ben's rules are Fizz 1's, checked against eighteen traces that Fizz 1's own simulation wrote ([chapter 8](chapter:rules)). The chicken clucks with a caption on both screens and a sound on both, placed in the stereo field by where the chicken is: channels 5 and 14 of an OPL3 on DOS, a stereo panner on the Web. `FIZZ2.EXE` has a probe, a start-up check and a help screen ([chapter 9](chapter:slice)). The slice's placeholder level music, "Crater Fields", plays on both. Both screens draw a small two-row HUD in Fizz 1's look (shots, score, deaths and a tray of key sockets), take Esc to a Paused menu and an Options screen, mute and darken the music under them, and draw everything Fizz in one accent colour, grape; DOS saves its settings in `FIZZ2.CFG` and chooses its frame rate with `/FPS30`, `/FPS60` or `/FPSAUTO`. The same replay ends `FINAL TICK {{golden.finalTick}} HASH {{golden.final}}` on native x86_64, WebAssembly and DOS. Milestone 4 is merged (#99 to #108): the rules raise an event for each jump, shot, snack and bounce, the level has a snack nook where each one happens, Fizz 1's whole caption table shows on both screens, and eleven more sound effects play with it, with a "Hear an effect" list on the Web ([chapter 10](chapter:effects)). After listening on both platforms the owner chose Fizz 1's own jump, pogo and fizz shot, so DOS plays Fizz 1 DOS's data for them; soda cans refill the blaster, and the replay is now 1,757 ticks. The owner has played the Web and DOS builds, heard the cluck, the music and the new Web effects and said Milestone 4 looks good in gameplay; nobody has reported hearing the menu mix, the can's sound or any sound on real hardware, and no 486 has run `FIZZ2.EXE`.

**Milestone 2 has started** ([chapter 11](chapter:pipeline)). The Fizz Asset Pack format is specified (`docs/FAP.md`), the `fizz-pack` reader decodes it with no allocator, and OPX, an open format for OPL register streams, is specified as a draft with no reader yet. `fizz-bake` and the loaders are planned. The DOS checks now run in groups, so a pull request runs only the ones its paths can affect.

Each chapter ends with three shelves: *catalogued* entries name the check behind them, *uncatalogued* entries are built and not verified, and the *wish list* is planned. Tags such as *merged* and *in review* come from `gh` when the site is built. The roadmap page groups the work by milestone, the manual page shows the player's manual with the evidence behind each section, the gallery collects every figure, and "How this archive is made" gives the commands that regenerate the site.
